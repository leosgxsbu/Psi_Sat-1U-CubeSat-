Six-Layer PCB Architecture

LEO-GRM internal stack — 95.0 × 95.0 mm decks

The LEO-GRM bus uses PCBs as load-bearing tiers inside a 100.0 × 100.0 × 113.5 mm aluminum chassis. Decks are spaced on M3 stainless threaded rods with dual 40-pin 2.54 mm stacking headers on the left and right edges for power and signal distribution.

Current build status: the team is designing the board stack. The STM32 flight computer board (Stage 3) schematic is nearly complete. Next, we plan a low‑cost bench test of the CsI(Tl) crystal + SiPM detector and a development board for flight software, before any flight hardware.

Stage 1

Science Payload Deck

95.0 × 95.0 mm

  • 50 × 50 × 3 mm CsI(Tl) scintillator, 0.1 mm ESR wrap
  • Hamamatsu S13360-6050PE SiPM (JLC C42451657)
  • PMAC: Nd magnet + mu-metal hysteresis rods
  • Shielded 3-pin analog jumper off main stacking bus

Stage 2

Front-End Signal Digitization

95.0 × 95.0 mm

  • LTC6227HMS8 op-amp (LCSC C2924151)
  • C-L-C π-filter on +53 V SiPM bias
  • 2–10 mV analog pulse → 3.3 V logic square wave

Stage 3

Command & Data Handling

95.0 × 95.0 mm

  • STM32F411RET6 @ 100 MHz (LCSC C61314)
  • EXTI10 on PA10, 1 µs timestamp resolution
  • 45 min post-deploy RF silence (firmware)

Stage 4

EPS Power Manager

95.0 × 95.0 mm

  • TI BQ25713 buck-boost MPPT solar charger
  • GaAs cells on exterior PCB facets
  • +3.3 V and +5.0 V regulated rails

Stage 5

Battery & UHF Ballast

95.0 × 95.0 mm

  • 2× 18650 LiFePO₄ 2S1P (Keystone 1042 clips)
  • Adafruit RFM95W — AX.25 @ 9600 baud GFSK

Stage 6

Antenna Deployment Deck

95.0 × 95.0 mm

  • 16.3 cm tape-measure dipole elements (437 MHz)
  • 10 Ω / 1 W burn resistor + nylon restraint line
  • IRLML2502 MOSFET thermal release actuator
